# Telework policy (internal staff)

Staff with a structural telework agreement may work from home up to three days per week.

Staff working from home at least two days per week receive a monthly home-office allowance of EUR 140, paid
with the monthly salary.

Equipment: a laptop and headset are provided. A second screen can be requested through the IT portal.
