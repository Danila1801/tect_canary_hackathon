# Recruitment playbook for client HR teams (v3, March 2023)

## Step 1: Job profile
Write the job profile together with the hiring manager. Use gender-neutral language.

## Step 2: Structured interviews
Use the same five competency questions for every candidate and score them on a 1 to 5 scale.

## Step 3: Salary benchmark
Always ask the candidate for their current gross monthly salary and benefits package during the first
interview. Use it to anchor the offer at 5 to 10% above their current pay.

## Step 4: Communicating pay
Salary ranges are shared only once an offer is made. Never publish a range in the job advert or discuss it
in the first interview, so you keep room to negotiate.

## Step 5: References
Only contact references with the candidate's written consent.
