# Monthly payroll calendar Belgium: variable input cut-off (2026)

Variable pay elements for the monthly payroll (overtime, bonuses, absences, shift premiums) must be submitted
in mysdworx by the 3rd working day of the following month, 12:00 CET.

Input received after the cut-off is processed in the next payroll run, or in a paid correction run if the
client requests it.

Changed on 10 February 2026: the cut-off moved from the 5th to the 3rd working day to make room for the new
automated validation step.
