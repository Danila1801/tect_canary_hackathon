# Onboarding checklist: new employee at a client (Belgium)

1. Receive the signed employment contract.
2. Submit the Dimona IN declaration at the latest at the moment the employee starts work.
3. Collect the bank account number and a copy of the ID card.
4. Check the joint committee (paritair comite) of the client and the matching wage scale.
5. Create the employee in mysdworx and send the self-service invitation.
