Thomas, for cross-border workers there are always two separate questions. Social security: apply the
multi-state rules and check the 25% threshold in the country of residence, then request an A1. Wage tax: look
at the treaty and at the tolerance for home-working days, which changes regularly, so check the current number
with me before telling the client anything. Please log these cases with me, we have no official article yet.
