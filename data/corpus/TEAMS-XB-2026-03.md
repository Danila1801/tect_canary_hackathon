[18 Mar 2026] Ann Peeters: Client asks: employee lives in Belgium, works 70% in the Netherlands and 30% in
Belgium. Which social security applies? Can't find anything on the wiki.

[18 Mar 2026] Jonas Verbeke: First check whether 25% or more of working time or pay is in the country of
residence, so Belgium. If yes, Belgian social security applies to all of it and you request an A1 certificate
through the Belgian institution. Wage tax is a separate question and is split by working days under the BE-NL
treaty. Send me the contract, don't guess on these.
