Hi Els,

Short answer: no, please stop. Since the pay transparency rules apply from June 2026, your recruiters should
no longer ask candidates about their current or previous salary. You also need to share the pay range, or the
starting pay, before the interview, not at offer stage.

On the contracts: the confidentiality clause on salary (article 9 in the 2022 template) can no longer be
enforced, because employees are allowed to disclose their own pay. I would remove it from new contracts.

I checked both points with Sofie Claes from Legal last week.
