# Demo video script (under 3 minutes)

## Before you record

1. Start the app (`npm run dev`) and open http://localhost:3000.
2. Sign in as **Ann Peeters**. Get the password from Danil.
3. Set the browser zoom to 90% so a whole answer fits on screen.
4. Record with **Win + Shift + R** (Snipping Tool, record a region) or **Win + Alt + R** (Game Bar, records the
   current window). On a Mac: **Cmd + Shift + 5**.
5. Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** and paste the link in Builderbase.

Tip: record the screen first, then record the voice over it. Speak slowly. It's fine if it's 2:40.

## The script

**0:00, Ask tab, empty. The hook.**
> "A client calls Ann: *can our recruiters still ask candidates what they earn?* Ann searches. The playbook says:
> always ask. But since the 7th of June, that's illegal. A normal AI assistant finds that playbook and repeats the
> wrong answer, confidently."

**0:20, still the Ask tab. The idea.**
> "SD Worx already has a search assistant and a tool that tracks new laws. What's missing is the link between
> them: when the law changes, which of our own documents just became wrong, and who fixes them? That's Canary."

**0:30, click the first example question.**
> "Verified answer: no. And here's *why* Ann can rely on it: the exact sentence from the legal update, with a trust
> score of 96, and a colleague's email that agrees. Here's what Canary *ignored*: the old playbook, outdated by
> law, and a Teams message where someone hid instructions for the AI. That message never reached the model. And
> Marc, who owns the playbook, gets a heads-up."

**1:05, click the second example question.**
> "Now the payroll deadline. The wiki says the 3rd working day, the help centre says the 5th. Canary doesn't pick
> a side. It shows both, ignores the Dutch page because it's another country, and sends it to Koen, because the
> help-centre article has no owner."

**1:30, Detect tab.**
> "This is the view for the people who own the documents. One legal change: five wrong statements in three
> documents, each with a suggested rewrite and an owner. And here's the deadline conflict, with a ticket that
> proves it already delayed overtime pay for 37 employees."

**1:50, click Approve on Marc's playbook card (you are still Ann).**
> "Ann can't approve Marc's document. The server checks who owns it. The owner decides, not the AI."

**2:00, scroll to the "Only in chats" card.**
> "Cross-border payroll only lives in three chats by Jonas. There's no official page. Canary drafts the article
> from his own words, and Jonas only has to approve it."

**2:15, Trust tab. Click one document.**
> "Every trust score is a formula you can read. How official, how old, who owns it, conflicts, legal changes. No
> black box."

**2:30, back to the Ask tab. The close.**
> "The AI finds, the code decides. In our tests: 7 out of 7 problems found, zero false alarms, every fact traced
> to an exact sentence. Next step: plug Canary in between Legal Watch and the assistant SD Worx already has. From
> *I found something* to *I know why I can trust it*."
